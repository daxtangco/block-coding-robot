    Train Your Own Detection Model
    
                        
    Upload an image dataset, train a detection model on it, and the robot will use your model to recognize objects. Detection runs on the server (PC); the camera just supplies the picture.
    

                        
    
                            
    📥 1. Get a dataset
    
                            
    Download a free, open-source dataset and export it in “YOLOv8” format (this gives you a .zip containing a data.yaml file). Good sources:
    
                            
      
                                
    • Roboflow Universe — universe.roboflow.com (thousands of ready datasets; choose Download → YOLOv8)
      • 
                                
    • Kaggle Datasets — kaggle.com/datasets
      • 
                                
    • Google Open Images — storage.googleapis.com/openimages
      •
